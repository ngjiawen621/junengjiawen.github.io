💼 About Me

I recently graduated on 25 July 2025 from the University of Melbourne with a Bachelor of Commerce (Finance). Before that, I completed my first year at HELP University in Malaysia.

I’m grateful to have experienced two different learning environments and a variety of teaching styles. With this degree, I’m ready to step into the real world, keep learning, and build practical skills along the way.

I know I’m not at 100% yet (who is, really?), but I’m eager to take that first step — and learn fast. Through internships, I’ve worked in healthcare, finance, and tech — and I genuinely enjoyed each one. Every role gave me something new, whether through projects, mentors, or colleagues who became friends.

Some mentors I still remember fondly: Silas, Hazel, Soon Shuen, Wei Lik, Greg… the list goes on. I met each of them through short work experiences or spontaneous opportunity chats. What they all had in common? They never discouraged questions — they welcomed curiosity and exploration.

I once asked a colleague from a different department what it was like being a business analyst. She was surprised — I had no background in it and wasn’t even in her team. But I was genuinely curious. I didn’t want a textbook answer — I wanted to hear from someone living it.

My takeaway? Keep trying. Don’t get comfortable too early. Explore more fields. Get hands-on experience. Talk to people. They’ll teach you more than any lecture ever could — and yes, sometimes they’ll challenge you too. But that’s how you grow, and that’s how you find your path.

🎵 My Musical Journey

I’ve been surrounded by music since I was three — starting with the piano. Not in a competitive sense, but more in a playful, creative way. At 13, I picked up the violin and joined my high school band, where we played everything from film scores to musical theatre classics.

Even during lockdown, our ensemble stayed connected. Just before everything shut down, we managed to record and release a performance together — a small but proud memory.

These days, I also dabble in guitar and ukulele. I love how music brings people together — nothing beats the feeling of making music in sync with others. One day, I hope to form a little duet or band... maybe even try busking in Melbourne (we’ll see!).

🍳 Cooking Experiments

I won’t pretend to be a master chef, but I do have a curious palate. I’ve tackled some pretty specific (and ambitious) dishes — like sambal matah and roti canai. Definitely not your beginner recipes!

As an international student in Melbourne, I slowly fell in love with cooking. Before that? I was completely lost in the kitchen. Don’t even ask me how to cook a chicken — I had no idea. But now? I can proudly make scrambled and sunny side up eggs like a pro. Small wins count!

🪴 Plant Parent Life

When I moved into a place with a balcony, I finally had space to start my plant journey. These days, I proudly care for:

  • Mon Mon — my Monstera Deliciosa
  • Wang Wang — my Chinese Money Plant (which means “prosper” in Mandarin)
  • Hijau — my first-ever Snake Plant (which means “green” in Malay)

I check their leaves for stress, water them gently (but not too much!), and beam with pride every time a new leaf appears. Hijau’s due for a repotting soon — wish me luck!
